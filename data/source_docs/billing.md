# Billing and Plans

The service has Free, Pro, and Enterprise plans. Pro subscriptions are billed monthly or annually. Annual Pro subscriptions receive a 15 percent discount compared with paying monthly for twelve months.

Workspace owners can view invoices from Settings > Billing > Invoices. Invoices are available as PDF files after a payment has been processed.

A workspace can downgrade from Pro to Free at any time. The downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing period, so paid features remain available until that date. Enterprise contract changes are handled through the account team.
