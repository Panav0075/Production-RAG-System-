# Team Permissions

A workspace has Owner, Admin, Member, and Viewer roles. Owners control billing, workspace deletion, and ownership transfer. Admins can manage members and most workspace settings but cannot transfer ownership.

Members can create and edit projects they are allowed to access. Viewers have read-only access and cannot modify project content.

Only Owners and Admins can invite new workspace members. Invitations expire after seven days. An expired invitation must be replaced with a new invitation.

Project-level access can further restrict which projects a Member or Viewer can open. Workspace roles and project permissions are evaluated together.
